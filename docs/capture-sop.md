# Staff capture SOP — five shots (v1)

Try-on does **not** invent a sari from a collage. The model wears the **full hanging / draped product photo**. The other four shots exist so pallu, border, body, and blouse stay the same SKU.

A SKU appears on the kiosk only after staff **approve**.

## The five photographs

1. **`full_hanging`** — entire sari hanging, laid flat, or draped on a dummy. Even light, taut cloth, border and pallu visible. **This is the garment image for Stage B.**
2. **`pallu`** — decorative end, motif sharp. Fail if the pallu is folded over itself.
3. **`body_field`** — the field/body metres (what wraps torso and legs).
4. **`border`** — running border along the long edge (zari/motif repeat). One clear strip beats two blurry ones.
5. **`blouse`** — blouse piece, or flag “customer keeps her own blouse.”

Optional: pallu end-border close-up, blouse back, colour card + ruler.

## Pipeline

1. Quality gate (blur, glare, border hidden by folds).
2. Store raw parts.
3. Stage A: if `full_hanging` exists, that file **is** the reconstructed garment. Do not replace it with a labeled collage.
4. Staff approve only if pallu and border match the cloth in hand.
5. Kiosk lists approved SKUs. Stage B drapes `full_hanging` onto the customer still.

## Honest language

Generated stills are **how it would look**. The physical sari is the source of truth for hand-feel.
